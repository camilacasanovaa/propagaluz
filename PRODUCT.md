# Propagaluz · Product

The single source of truth for what propagaluz is, who it serves, and how it operates. Every decision made elsewhere in this project refers back here.

## What it is
A foundation based in Madrid, raising funds for the education of underprivileged Venezuelan children — ages 3 to 20 — and championing the freedom of speech of Venezuelans, young and old. Propagaluz exists to spread light: education as light, voice as light, attention as light.

## Why it exists
Propagaluz is the continuation of a family vocation. Two generations of Camila Casanova's family used the printed word in Venezuela to insist on truth.

- **Eladio Alemán Sucre** (great-grandfather) founded *El Bolígrafo*, which later became *Propagaluz*, and went on to found *El Carabobeño*.
- **Eduardo Alemán Pérez** ("Opa," grandfather) built *El Carabobeño* into a nationally significant newspaper that took a stand against government corruption.

The foundation's name carries Opa inside it — pr**OPA**galuz — and propagates the same instinct in a new form: where the family poured ink, this generation pours opportunity into the schooling and the voices of Venezuelan children.

(The internal historical record lives in `LEGACY.md` and `research/legacy/`. The public-facing surface of this legacy is shaped, not literal — see `BRAND.md`.)

## Who it serves
Underprivileged Venezuelan children, ages 3 to 20, whose access to education is limited or interrupted. Propagaluz does not select beneficiaries directly; the partner organization on the ground does the selection and the delivery.

## How it works (theory of change · v0 draft)
Propagaluz is the **bridge** between a diaspora and home: it gathers donations from individuals and organizations outside Venezuela, vets and selects one or more partner organizations doing direct educational work on the ground, and channels the funds with full accounting. The foundation's value-add is **trust, story, and access** — making the work legible and reachable to a community of donors that institutional fundraising could not reach on its own.

(Currently exploring Fe y Alegría as a primary partner — see `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.)

## Geography
- **Operations base:** Madrid, Spain.
- **Beneficiary geography:** Venezuela, exclusively, for now.
- **Event geography:** Madrid first; Miami and other diaspora hubs welcome over time.
- **Founder context:** born in Miami (US citizen), raised in Madrid 11+ years (Spanish citizen), Venezuelan family.

## What the visitor takes away
A first-time visitor to propagaluz.org leaves having **read the manifesto and felt the mission**. Donation is the natural next step, never the headline. Comprehension before commitment.

## The standard we hold
Every gesture from propagaluz is deliberate. Every page, every word, every interaction earns its place. The foundation is for something — for the unfinished schooling of a girl in Maracaibo, for the unheard voice of a boy in Petare, for the long arc of a family vocation that refuses to end. The standard is set by the children we serve, not by the conventions of the sector.
