# Propagaluz · Manifesto

A child with a school, a book, and a voice changes more in one life than any page changes in a century.

We believe this because we have seen it. For almost a hundred years, the names of this family have been written in ink, on paper, on the conviction that a word in its proper place is a form of light. We continue the same vocation today, in a different medium.

We believe in education as a form of light. A class that begins. A book that opens. A question a child dares to ask out loud. Each of these propagates.

We believe that a Venezuelan girl has the right to read, to study, to think, and to say what she thinks. That sequence, in that order, is the difference between a future and a long wait.

We believe in propagation as a method. One school lights another. One teacher lights another. One girl lights something in her parents, her siblings, her block. To propagate is the verb. To multiply is the consequence.

We gather what we can, from where we can, and we hand it to the people already doing the work in Venezuela. So that every euro, every dollar, every bolívar lands where a girl is waiting to begin to read.

This foundation belongs to the children it serves. It is a promise kept in the name of those who taught us, for nearly a century, that the word matters: in any format, in any era.

**Propagaluz. Spread the light.**
