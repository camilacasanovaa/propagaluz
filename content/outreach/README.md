# Outreach · partner introductions

Two drafted emails for first contact with the foundation's intended partners. Both written in Spanish, both from Camila Casanova with Andrés Casanova Vegas in copy as legal officer.

## What's here

- `entreculturas.es.md` — Fundación Entreculturas, Madrid. Fe y Alegría's Spanish affiliate. The education pillar.
- `cecodap.es.md` — CECODAP, Caracas. Venezuelan children's-rights organization. The voice pillar.

## Before sending — checklist

1. **Get a propagaluz email address.** Two paths:
   - **Strongly preferred:** register the domain (propagaluz.org, .es, or .com) and create `camila@propagaluz.org` plus `andres@propagaluz.org`. Domain registration: ~12€/year. Mailbox: free with most domain registrars or via Google Workspace (~6€/user/month).
   - **Workable for v1:** send from Camila's personal email with a clean "From" name set to *Camila Casanova · Propagaluz*. Less ideal because the email reads as personal rather than institutional, but acceptable while the domain is being registered.
2. **Find CECODAP's contact email.** It is not on the contact page in obvious form. Three paths to try, in order:
   - Visit `cecodap.org`, look at the *Contacto* page; if there is a contact form, use it (paste the email body in).
   - Look for an email on their LinkedIn page or in their published reports (often `cecodap@cecodap.org` or `comunicaciones@cecodap.org`).
   - If neither works, send a short message via their Instagram or X account asking for the right email address for a partnership inquiry.
3. **Replace the placeholders.** Both emails contain `[correo de contacto]`, `[teléfono]`, and `[correo de Andrés Casanova Vegas]`. Fill them.
4. **Andrés reviews the drafts.** He is named as legal officer in both. He needs to read both before they go out and approve his contact details.

## Recommended sequence

**Send Entreculturas first.** They are the primary partner (education is the lead pillar). Wait one full week for a response before sending the CECODAP email.

**Then send CECODAP.** If Entreculturas has already replied, the CECODAP email gains weight from being able to say *"Estamos en conversaciones con Entreculturas para el pilar educativo."* (line is already in the draft).

Sending both on the same day is fine if you're impatient. The week of waiting is preferable for two reasons: (a) it lets the Entreculturas reply shape how you talk to CECODAP, (b) it keeps you from juggling two simultaneous first conversations as a 17-year-old founder with school and a life.

## Follow-up cadence

- **No response after 7 days:** send a short, polite follow-up. Two-line message:
  > *Buenos días, escribo para retomar el mensaje de la semana pasada. Adjunto la propuesta original por si se traspapeló. Quedo a su disposición. — Camila*
- **No response after a second follow-up (so 14 days from first send):** stop. Move on. Note in `DECISIONS.md` and revisit in a quarter.
- **Positive response:** propose **two or three concrete time slots** for a video call (Zoom or Google Meet). Have Andrés on the call with you. Prepare a one-page document beforehand summarizing what propagaluz is, what you want from the partnership, and what you are bringing (this is downstream work; do not draft yet).

## What NOT to do in this email cycle

- Do **not** send funds or sign anything in the first conversation. The first conversation is exploratory.
- Do **not** promise propagaluz will support a specific program publicly until they confirm interest.
- Do **not** publish the partner names on the website or social until the agreement is signed in writing.
- Do **not** mention politics, regimes, or specific Venezuelan officials at any point. The whole framing is about the work and the children. Both organizations live this rule themselves.

## After both first conversations

Whether one, both, or neither says yes, log the outcome in `DECISIONS.md` and update `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`. If both say yes, the next operational steps are: drafting a partnership agreement (probably needs a lawyer), and writing the "Where the money goes" page on the website that names them publicly.
