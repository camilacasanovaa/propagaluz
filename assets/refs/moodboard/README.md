# Moodboard — what propagaluz should feel like

This folder is where you drop screenshots, photos, references, anything visual that captures what you want propagaluz to feel like. **I don't write a single line of code until this is rich enough to design from.**

## How to use this folder

1. **Take screenshots however you normally do.** `Cmd+Shift+4` on Mac → drag a region. Or save images from anywhere.
2. **Drag them into this folder.** `~/Documents/PROJECTS/propagaluz/assets/refs/moodboard/`. Filenames don't matter — call them whatever.
3. **After each drop, tell me what each one is FOR** in our chat. One line. Examples:
   - *"Hero treatment I like — the way the photo is dark and the type is white."*
   - *"This font is exactly the kind of headline that calls attention."*
   - *"This donation button is what ours should look like."*
   - *"These three colors together feel right."*
4. **No rush.** Drop in batches over hours or days. Each batch I'll catalog and you'll see the picture sharpen.

- **Studios** — *Pentagram*, *Athletics*, *Mucho*, *Order*
- **Other foundations** — *charity:water*, *heartsinmotion.events*, *maxstrong.org* (you already named these)
- **Bookstores** — *McNally Jackson*, *Phaidon*, *Hatchards*
- **Photographers** — Whoever's photography style you want propagaluz photos to feel like
- **Books and posters** — Book jackets, exhibition posters, vinyl covers
- **Any random thing** — A subway sign, a museum label, a coffee bag, a font on a t-shirt

If it stops you and you feel something, it belongs here.

## What I most need refs of

In rough order of priority. Don't worry about hitting all of these — just go where your eye goes.

### 1 · Hero treatments (the top of a page)
Three to five examples of how a page should open. Photo? Type-only? Split? Overlay? What scale does the headline live at? What does the donate button look like above the fold?

### 2 · Typography
What kind of headline type stops you? What kind of body text? You said *"fonts that call attention"* — show me what calls to you. **Magazine covers are gold here.**

### 3 · Photography style
What kind of photos should propagaluz use? Documentary? Posed portraits? Hands? Light through a window? Black-and-white? Saturated? Natural light? Show me three to five photo styles whose feel you want.

### 4 · Overall vibe — whole sites or pages
One to three sites whose *entire feel* you want propagaluz to be in the family of. Not just one section — the whole posture, the whole mood.

### 5 · Color
Two or three palettes that feel right. Doesn't have to be from a website — could be a painting, an interior, a photograph, a brand identity. **Color you find beautiful in real life often translates better than color you choose from a swatch.**

### 6 · Donation UX
How should the donation widget feel and look? Charity:water has a strong pattern. There are others. Show me what you'd actually click.

### 7 · Section transitions and rhythm
How do sections divide on a page you love? Hard color blocks? Whitespace? A photo break? A single ruling line? Show me how a page should breathe.

## What I'll do with this

Once you've dropped a substantive batch (call it ten to twenty references), I'll:

1. Catalog every image with my read of what it's saying.
2. Identify the *visual language* that emerges from your refs — patterns, themes, repetitions.
3. Build a single static visual mockup of the homepage that synthesizes your refs.
4. You approve the mockup or push back at the mockup stage.
5. Then I build code against the approved mockup.

**No more iterating through code. We iterate through reference.**

## When you're done collecting

Just say *"the moodboard is ready"* (or *"start cataloging"*). I'll go through everything you dropped and we'll talk about what I'm seeing before any design happens.
