Our intention

A word in its proper place sets something alight.

We exist for the children of Venezuela whose childhood is waiting. For the curiosity that is bigger than the access. For the generation growing up in pause, with the intelligence intact and the opportunity half-given.

Where it lands

One foundation.
All of our light.

Propagaluz exists to carry resources from outside Venezuela to an organization already doing the work. They sustain the classrooms, pay the teachers, put books in the hands of children who would not otherwise have them. Our proceeds go to them.

Fe y Alegría

A movement of popular education that began in 1955 in a home in a working-class Caracas neighborhood, with 100 students. Today it works in 22 countries.

In Venezuela they run 350 education centers and 22 educational radio stations in the country’s most vulnerable communities, reaching more than 100,000 students.

Since 1955 · 350 education centers

Propagaluz is an independent initiative. We are not partners of Fe y Alegría and are not endorsed by them. We name them only to tell you where our proceeds go.

June 2026 · Earthquake relief

When the ground shook, we turned toward it.
