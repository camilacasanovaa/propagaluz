Our intention

A word in its proper place sets something alight.

We exist for the children of Venezuela whose childhood is waiting. For the curiosity that is bigger than the access. For the generation growing up in pause, with the intelligence intact and the opportunity half-given.

Where it lands

Fe y Alegría.

Two hundred schools in the country’s hardest places. They have stayed open through everything. Our role is to help them stay open.

Fe y Alegría

A movement of popular education that began as a single school in a poor Caracas neighborhood and has grown into a presence across more than twenty countries. In Venezuela it remains the largest independent network of schools serving children at the margins.

They run more than two hundred schools across the country’s most vulnerable communities: paying teachers, feeding children, providing books and curriculum where the need is greatest. Their work spans preschool through technical training, and reaches hundreds of thousands of children who would otherwise have no consistent education at all.
