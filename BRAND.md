# Propagaluz · Brand

How propagaluz sounds, looks, and behaves. The rules in this file override taste calls.

## Voice — three words
1. **Defiant.** Quietly. The defiance shows in the standard we hold, not in slogans. Defiant against indifference, against the slow attrition of a child's potential, against the conventions of the sector. Never defiant against a named opponent in public copy.
2. **Child-oriented.** Every decision answers to a 12-year-old in Caracas. If she can't feel it, it's wrong. This governs reading level, visual scale, color saturation, motion vocabulary.
3. **Devoted.** Sustained, long-term care. Not a campaign, not a trend, a vocation. The work compounds over decades.

## Language rules (public copy)
- **No negatives.** "We refuse X" becomes "Every gesture is deliberate." Anti-patterns are stated as standards we hold, not as things we reject. The foundation is FOR something, always.
- **No politics, named.** Freedom of speech is framed as **the right to be heard**, not as opposition to any regime. Education is framed as **light**, not as a response to neglect. We do not name parties, leaders, or governments.
- **No em-dashes.** Use a period, a comma, or a colon.
- **No roman numerals.** Ever.
- **No corp-speak, no dev jargon.** A grandmother and a 12-year-old must both be able to read every page.
- **Spanish and English are first-class.** Spanish is the mother tongue. English is the diaspora. Neither is a translation of the other; each is written natively.
- **Exclamation marks are reserved for direct quotes from beneficiaries.** Never in editorial copy.

## Internal copy (this folder, decisions, research)
The language rules above apply to **public** surfaces. Internal documents may name politics, may use negatives, may carry the full nuance of what we are building. Honesty internally is the price of grace externally.

## Decide-don't-ask · taste sheet
Calls made (or deliberately deferred). The next round of work settles what is open.

| Element | Decision |
|---|---|
| Color palette | Deferred until visual direction round. |
| Typography | Deferred until visual direction round. |
| Motion vocabulary | Restrained, intentional. Never decorative. |
| Photography | Real, never stock. If we cannot get real, we use type and color. |
| Illustration | Avoided unless commissioned from a Venezuelan artist. |
| Tone of imagery | Children pictured with dignity. Never as objects of pity. |
| Donation framing | The donation is a gesture of belief, not a transaction of charity. |

## Anti-patterns (internal — guide the work, never appear in public copy)
- White-saviorism in any form.
- "Help us help them."
- Pity-based asks, sad-piano-music energy.
- Charity-graphics tropes: hands holding earth, silhouetted children, gradient hearts.
- Generic non-profit template aesthetics.
- Generic AI UI: gradient blobs without purpose, "join the revolution" CTAs, glassmorphism for its own sake.
- Bake sales, candy raffles, the student-foundation aesthetic.

## Partners and naming
Partner organization names and logos (Fe y Alegría, or any future partner) appear in public copy only after a written agreement permits it. Until then, describe the work, not the partner.

## The "OPA" inside the name
The name propagaluz preserves Opa — Camila's grandfather — inside it. This is a private intimacy, not a marketing point. It surfaces in copy only where it earns its place (likely the About page), and never as a logo treatment or visual gimmick.
